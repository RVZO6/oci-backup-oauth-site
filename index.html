OCI Backup Sync

This site describes a personal Google OAuth application, OCI Backup Sync, used by its owner for private command-line automation.

OCI Backup Sync (rclone)

The dedicated backup client connects rclone to the owner's Google Drive to store encrypted Restic backup repositories and synchronize their retention policy. Backup contents are not published by this site.

The Google consent screen identifies the Google Drive permissions requested by this client.

This is a personal integration, not a public service or a Google product.
